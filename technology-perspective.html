Start with purpose

Before choosing a tool, define the problem. A clear purpose makes it easier to distinguish useful innovation from complexity. The question is not simply what technology can do, but what it should help people do better.

Design around people

A digital service should be understandable to the people who use it. Access, clarity, privacy, and reliability deserve attention alongside features. Thoughtful design begins with listening and continues through feedback.

Make room to learn

Small, considered steps can create room to evaluate an idea before extending it. This is a perspective on innovation, rather than a description of a specific Wassail product or project.
